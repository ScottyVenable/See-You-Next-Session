## 1. Art Assets (2D Sprites - Clip Studio Paint)

### 1.1 Character: The Patient (Base Layers)
We need a modular character system to layer symptoms on top.

*   **Base Body (Male):** Seated pose, neutral expression.
*   **Base Body (Female):** Seated pose, neutral expression.
*   **Clothing Sets:**
    *   Casual/Messy (Sweatpants, stained shirt).
    *   Manic/Flashy (Bright colors, jewelry, mismatched).
    *   Professional/Clean (Suit or button-up).

### 1.2 Symptom Overlays (Transparent PNGs)
These sit on top of the Base Body and are toggled on/off.
*   [ ] **Eyes:** Bags under eyes (Dark circles).
*   [ ] **Eyes:** Dilated pupils (Wide).
*   [ ] **Eyes:** Red/Bloodshot.
*   [ ] **Hands:** Trembling/Blur effect.
*   [ ] **Hands:** Bitten nails/Bandages on fingers.
*   [ ] **Mouth:** Chewed lip (Slight redness/scab).
*   [ ] **General:** Sweat drops (Forehead).

### 1.3 The Office (Background)
*   [ ] **Main Desk View:** First-person perspective looking across a desk.
*   [ ] **Background Wall:** Neutral therapy office. Bookshelf, diploma, plant.
*   [ ] **Lighting:** Standard warm office lighting.

### 1.4 User Interface (UI)
*   [ ] **Dialogue Box:** Paper texture or clean medical aesthetic.
*   [ ] **Clipboard:** A literal clipboard sprite to hold tokens.
*   [ ] **Token Icons:**
    *   Text Token: A small slip of paper.
    *   Visual Token: An eye icon or polaroid frame.
*   [ ] **Focus Meter:** A glass tube or vertical bar.
    *   Fill texture (Teal liquid).
*   [ ] **Handbook:** Closed book icon + Open book overlay background.
*   [ ] **Clock:** Simple analog clock face.

## 2. Audio Assets (FL Studio)

### 2.1 Sound Effects (SFX)
*   [ ] **UI_Hover:** Soft paper rustle.
*   [ ] **UI_Click:** Pen click or stamp sound.
*   [ ] **Token_Pickup:** Paper lifting sound.
*   [ ] **Token_Drop:** Paper slap on wood.
*   [ ] **Focus_Mode_Enter:** Deep breath or low hum (underwater effect).
*   [ ] **Focus_Mode_Exit:** Exhale.
*   [ ] **Scribble:** Pencil writing (short and long variations).
*   [ ] **Breakthrough (Success):** "Ding" or chime (Positive feedback).
*   [ ] **Error (Fail):** Dull thud or discordant note.

### 2.2 Music / Ambience
*   [ ] **Office Ambience:** Very quiet AC hum, distant ticking clock. (Loopable).
*   [ ] **Tension Track:** Subtle low synth drone that fades in when Focus is low.
