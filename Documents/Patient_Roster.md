**Name:** [TBD]  
**Difficulty:** Easy  
**Disorder:** Generalized Anxiety Disorder (GAD)

### Profile
*   **Presentation:** Nervous, fidgety, tired.
*   **Key Dialogue:**
    *   "I just can't turn my brain off at night."
    *   "I feel like something bad is about to happen, even when I'm safe."
*   **Visual Symptoms (Focus Clues):**
    *   **Bitten Nails:** (Hands)
    *   **Bags under eyes:** (Face)
    *   **Chewed Lip:** (Face)
    *   **Looking Around:** (Head Animation/Pose)

### Contradiction Puzzle
*   **Statement:** "I'm sleeping fine, really."
*   **Visual:** *Bags under eyes* OR *Fatigue*.
*   **Result:** Patient admits to insomnia.

## Patient 2: The Challenge
**Name:** [TBD]  
**Difficulty:** Medium  
**Disorder:** Bipolar 1 Disorder

### Profile
*   **Presentation:** Variable. Starts Manic, shifts to Depressed (or vice versa) in later turns.
*   **Key Dialogue (Manic):**
    *   "I don't need sleep, I have so many ideas!"
    *   "I just bought a boat. It's going to be great."
*   **Key Dialogue (Depressed):**
    *   "What's the point?"
    *   "I'm just so tired."

### Visual Symptoms (Focus Clues)
*   **Manic Phase:**
    *   **Dilated Pupils:** (Eyes)
    *   **Flashy Clothes/Jewelry:** (Body)
    *   **Rapid Movement:** (Animation)
*   **Depressed Phase:**
    *   **Slumped Posture:** (Body)
    *   **Flat Expression:** (Face)
    *   **Messy Hair:** (Head)

### Contradiction Puzzle
*   **Statement:** "I'm just naturally high energy, I've always been like this."
*   **Visual:** *Crash/Slumped Posture* (in later turn) OR *History of depressive episodes* (if available in file).
*   **Result:** Patient acknowledges the cycle of highs and lows.

## Patient 3: The Boss (Stretch Goal)
**Name:** [TBD]  
**Difficulty:** Hard  
**Disorder:** Factitious Disorder

### Profile
*   **Presentation:** Overly dramatic about symptoms but physically healthy.
*   **Key Dialogue:**
    *   "I fell down the stairs, my leg is shattered."
    *   "I think the infection is spreading."

### Visual Symptoms (Focus Clues)
*   **Rosy Cheeks:** (Face - indicates health)
*   **Perfect Teeth/Hair:** (Head - indicates self-care)
*   **No Visible Wounds:** (Body)

### Contradiction Puzzle
*   **Statement:** "My leg is broken, I can't walk."
*   **Visual:** *Normal Reflexes* (or observed walking to chair).
*   **Result:** Patient becomes defensive or changes story.
